## Tips
- **Don't memorize solutions** — understand the pattern
- **If stuck > 20 min**, look at hints/solution, then re-implement from scratch
- **Track** in a spreadsheet: problem, pattern, time, confidence (1-5)
- **Re-solve** "red" problems (confidence ≤ 2) after 3 days
- **Mock interview** weekly on Pramp.com (free)
